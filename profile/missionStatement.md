# Mission Statement

Work in progress - below are an outline of our core values.

Writers Grove is commited to putting authors first.

We believe that Authors have full ownership and control of their work and are the only ones who should decide how that work is used.

We believe that our policies should be transparent and written out in plain language.

We believe in giving authors full creative freedom to write whatever type of works they desire.

We believe in protecting the privacy of authors.

We belive Authors should have a community that is safe and inclusive for all voices even those who do not agree with each other as long as the conduct remains respectful
